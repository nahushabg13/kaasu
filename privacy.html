Kaasu Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

In short: everything you enter in Kaasu stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your data anywhere. I never see it.

Who this policy is from

Kaasu is an expense tracker for Android, published on Google Play by Nahusha B G, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). Its package name is com.nahushabg.expenses. This policy covers the Kaasu app only.

What the app stores

Only what you type into it:

  • amounts, dates and notes for your income and expenses;
  • the categories you use, with their names and colours;
  • groups you create for shared expenses, and how each expense is split.

This is kept in a database in the app’s private storage on your device. Kaasu does not read your contacts, location, photos, messages or anything else on your phone, and it never asks you for a permission.

What I collect

Nothing. Kaasu has no servers and no user accounts, and I have added no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other third-party code to it. Because the app has no internet permission, Android itself stops it from connecting to the internet.

Google Play’s installer check

The copy of Kaasu you install from Google Play includes a check that Google Play adds to protect apps from being copied and redistributed. When the app opens, it asks the Play Store app on your phone to confirm that Kaasu was installed from Google Play. The check is part of Google Play, not something I wrote or control; it concerns the installation, not the records you keep in Kaasu, and it is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell, share or transfer your data, because I never receive it.

Backups

Kaasu can save a backup of your records as a file, into a folder you choose on your phone. It does this on its own about once a day, and whenever you ask it to in Settings → Backup. Those files stay on your device unless you move or share them yourself — Kaasu never uploads them anywhere, and cannot, because it has no internet permission. You can open, copy or delete them like any other file.

Android also has a backup feature of its own, run by Google rather than by Kaasu, which copies some apps’ data to your Google account. Kaasu currently has this switched off, so Android does not include Kaasu’s records in that backup. The only backups of your records are the files described above, in the folder you picked.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any entry, category or group inside the app.
  • To remove everything, clear Kaasu’s storage in your phone’s app settings, or uninstall the app. Both delete all Kaasu data from the device.
  • Backup files are not removed by uninstalling — that is the point of them. Delete them yourself from the folder you chose, using any file manager.

Security

Your data sits in storage that Android keeps private to Kaasu, so other apps cannot read it. Kaasu does not have its own lock or extra encryption, so anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and see your entries.

Children

Kaasu is not aimed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Kaasu ever changes how data is handled, for example by adding sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
