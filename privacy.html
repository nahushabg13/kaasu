Kaasu Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026

In short: everything you enter in Kaasu stays on your phone. The app has no account of its own, no ads and no analytics, and I never see your data. Kaasu makes no connection to the internet at all unless you choose to connect Google Drive for backups — and then it writes only to your own Drive, which only you can read.

Who this policy is from

Kaasu is an expense tracker for Android, published on Google Play by Nahusha B G, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). Its package name is com.nahushabg.expenses. This policy covers the Kaasu app only.

What the app stores

Only what you type into it:

  • amounts, dates and notes for your income and expenses;
  • the categories you use, with their names and colours;
  • groups you create for shared expenses, and how each expense is split.

This is kept in a database in the app’s private storage on your device. Kaasu does not read your contacts, location, photos, messages or anything else on your phone. The only permission it declares is internet access, used solely for the optional Google Drive backup described below; it asks for no other permission.

What I collect

Nothing. Kaasu has no servers and no user accounts, and I have added no analytics, crash reporting or advertising code to it. There is nowhere for your data to be sent to me, because no such place exists. If you connect Google Drive, your backups go directly from your phone to your Google account; they never pass through me, and I cannot read them.

Google Play’s installer check

The copy of Kaasu you install from Google Play includes a check that Google Play adds to protect apps from being copied and redistributed. When the app opens, it asks the Play Store app on your phone to confirm that Kaasu was installed from Google Play. The check is part of Google Play, not something I wrote or control; it concerns the installation, not the records you keep in Kaasu, and it is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell, share or transfer your data, because I never receive it.

Backups

On your phone. Kaasu saves a backup of your records as a file, into a folder you choose. It does this on its own about once a day, and whenever you ask it to in Settings → Local backup. Those files stay on your device unless you move or share them yourself, and you can open, copy or delete them like any other file.

In your Google Drive, only if you turn it on. Under Settings → Remote backup you can connect your Google account so a copy of each backup is also saved to a folder named “Kaasu” in your own Drive. This is off until you choose it, and you can disconnect at any time. Kaasu requests the narrowest Drive permission Google offers (drive.file), which lets it see only the files it created itself — it cannot read anything else in your Drive. The files go straight from your phone to your Google account. I have no access to them, and you can delete them from Drive yourself or revoke access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Android also has a backup feature of its own, run by Google rather than by Kaasu, which copies some apps’ data to your Google account automatically. Kaasu has this switched off, so Android does not include Kaasu’s records in it. The only backups of your records are the ones described above, which you set up yourself.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any entry, category or group inside the app.
  • To remove everything, clear Kaasu’s storage in your phone’s app settings, or uninstall the app. Both delete all Kaasu data from the device.
  • Backup files are not removed by uninstalling — that is the point of them. Delete them yourself from the folder you chose, using any file manager.
  • If you connected Google Drive, delete the “Kaasu” folder from your Drive, and revoke Kaasu’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

Your data sits in storage that Android keeps private to Kaasu, so other apps cannot read it. Kaasu does not have its own lock or extra encryption, so anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and see your entries.

Children

Kaasu is not aimed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Kaasu ever changes how data is handled, for example by adding sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
